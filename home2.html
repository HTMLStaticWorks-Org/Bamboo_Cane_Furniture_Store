How It Works

Our Meticulous Bamboo Process

1. Botanical Evaluation

We assess the bamboo species (Moso, Guadua, Timber), inspect fiber splitting, and provide a transparent estimate.

1
2

2. White-Glove Transport

Our crew provides padded pickup designed specifically to prevent fragile cane strands or aged bamboo nodes from flexing.

3. Structural Stabilization

We repair hollow stalk splits using concealed internal bamboo dowels and flexible tensile adhesives that breathe with moisture shifts.

3
4

4. Hand Re-Weaving

Certified weavers re-cane seats and backrests using sustainable rattan reeds soaked and tensioned to perfection.

5. Organic Seal & Polish

We hand-buff the piece with natural beeswax balms, organic tung oil, and UV moisture barriers that safeguard fiber flexibility.

5
6

6. Certified White-Glove Return

Your revived bamboo heirloom is returned with our 3-year warranty and a complimentary organic bamboo care kit.
