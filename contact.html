Frequently Asked Questions

Common Bamboo & Cane Enquiries

Quick answers regarding our custom bamboo commissions, repair timelines, pricing, and studio visits.

How do I get an estimate for bamboo repair or custom furniture?

You can fill out the Enquiry Form above or visit our Custom Order portal with photos of your piece. We evaluate the cane weave, node condition, and structural frame, and return a fixed-price written estimate within 24 business hours.

What is the typical turnaround time for hand-woven cane restoration?

Standard woven seat cane replacement usually takes 5 to 10 working days. Full bamboo structural rehabilitation and botanical oil re-curing takes 2 to 3 weeks depending on the drying and tension setting process.

Do you offer home pickup and delivery for large items?

Yes! We provide white-glove pickup and return delivery across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Long Island, and Northern New Jersey. Safe protective padded transport is guaranteed.

Can I visit the workshop to see bamboo materials and finishes in person?

Absolutely. Our Craftsville studio and gallery showroom are open Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Drop-ins are welcome, or schedule an appointment for dedicated consultations.
