Artisan Care Protocol

Seasonal Bamboo & Cane Care Checklist

Follow our master artisans' essential seasonal rules to ensure your bamboo joints never split and cane webbing stays taught and resilient.

Maintain 45%-60% Relative Humidity

Avoid placing bamboo directly near radiators or heat vents which dehydrate natural node membranes.

Semi-Annual Pure Beeswax Buffing

Rub organic beeswax along the grain to replenish lost botanical oils and preserve the warm amber sheen.

Moisture Compress for Sagging Caning

Place a damp cotton towel under sagging octagonal cane overnight to let the rattan fibers shrink back to tension.
