Established

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Bamboo & Cane Mastery

Founded in 1995, BambooCraft started as an artisan workshop dedicated to saving discarded vintage bamboo loungers and sagging cane chairs. Over the decades, our passion for natural fibers and sustainable wood alternatives has expanded into the country's foremost specialized bamboo furniture restoration laboratory.

Unlike conventional hardwood, hollow bamboo requires specialized radial tension clamping, node dowel pinning, and flexible non-toxic sealants that accommodate natural seasonal expansion. We treat every piece with the deep reverence that sustainable botanical materials deserve.

Our Mission

To resurrect and safeguard renewable bamboo furniture through time-honored cane weaving and certified non-toxic organic botanical finishes.

Our Vision

To lead the sustainable furniture revival, ensuring eco-friendly bamboo artifacts are cherished across family generations rather than sent to landfills.

Our Specialized Techniques

Traditional 7-Step Hand Caning

Hand-lacing individual strands of rattan through drilled hole perimeters to create resilient, flexible, and perfectly tensioned seating.

Steam Bending & Joint Clamping

Relaxing the lignin inside thick bamboo culms using controlled dry heat and steam to reshape warped frames and reset graceful curves.

Internal Node Pinning

Concealing solid hardwood or bamboo core dowels inside split stalks to reinforce structural load-bearing capacity without visible screws.

Our Sustainability Standards

  • Authentic Sourcing: Only sustainably harvested mature timber bamboo and top-grade Indonesian cane reed.
  • Non-Toxic Chemistry: Zero harsh petrochemical strippers or toxic solvents; we use citrus bio-solvents and food-safe oils.
  • Breathable Weatherproofing: Formulations that allow natural bamboo stalks to regulate moisture without blistering or peeling.
  • Full Transparency: Complete photographic condition logs, structural diagrams, and itemized restoration reports.
